Wedding App — Visual Direction

The look, before the build

Palette, type, and eight reference screens — Home, Venues, Vendor profile, Favorites, My Wedding, Profile — worked out per PROJECT_BRIEF.md. The vendor profile appears three times — a venue, a band, a dress salon — because only venues have capacity, a calendar and a street address. Where the venue page is carried by its availability calendar, every other category is carried by the vendor's own photographs. My Wedding is a proposed new tab, not yet in the brief: one slot per category for the vendor you've actually chosen, separate from the longer list of "maybes" in Favorites. Shown with real Zrenjanin-style content, in Serbian, since that's what a couple using the app would actually read.

Profile

Account basics and logout — exactly what the brief scoped, nothing more.

Adjusted after a look at Adriaticum Events' site and Instagram: their brand runs mostly white space with a thin-lined serif wordmark, and gold/blush show up as accents in the photography rather than filled UI blocks — so Home and the category list moved to photo-tile cards with a hairline gold rule instead of solid-color panels. Favorites, My Wedding, and Profile are new here — the bottom nav grew from 3 tabs to 4 to fit "My Wedding" in. System fonts stand in for Playfair Display / Inter here since the artifact sandbox can't load Google Fonts — the real app will pull both via expo-font and this exact palette will become the React Native Paper theme. Everything above is static: no taps, just the reference to build against.
